# Mist
A Native Dynamic Island for macOS at No Cost

Mist brings the iconic Dynamic Island experience to your Mac, displaying real-time system information directly in your menu bar.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6007ac6b-e446-40ce-b679-019413de626d

## Features

- **Dynamic Notch Display**: A sleek notch at the top of your screen that mimics the iPhone's Dynamic Island
- **Battery Status**: Monitor your Mac's battery percentage with visual indicators
- **Battery Alerts**: Automatic notifications when battery levels drop to critical levels (15%, 5%, 3%)

## Details

Mist runs in the background and displays a compact notch at the top center of your screen. The notch shows:
- **Compact View**: Minimal information while not hovered
- **Glance View**: Additional information appears on hover
- **Enlarged View**: Detailed system information available on tap
- **Status View**: Battery alert indicators when battery is low

## Requirements

- macOS 13.0 or later
- Apple Silicon

## Installation

1. Open the zip in the release
2. Extract the app from the zip
3. Drag the app into your applicaiton folder
